About DESKOBS

DESKOBS sells the small things that sit on a desk all day: mats, stands, lamps, chargers and bags. A desk is where most of us spend more hours than anywhere else, so these things should work quietly and look right.

How we pick

We keep the range small. Each object has to be well built, simple to use and useful every day. If we would not keep it on our own desk, it does not go in the shop.

How ordering works

  1. Add what you like to your bag and enter your delivery details.
  2. Send the order on WhatsApp, or pay by UPI.
  3. We confirm stock and delivery with you, then ship it.

Questions before you buy? Message us. Delivery and returns are explained on the shipping and returns page.
